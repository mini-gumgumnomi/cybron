## 🎯 Goal

By the end of this chapter you will:
- take **three full-length, 90-question mock exams** weighted like the real exam,
- analyze your results **domain by domain** and fix weaknesses,
- follow a **14-day final plan**,
- and walk into the test center (or open OnVUE) **calm and prepared** with an **exam-day checklist**.

---

## 🏠 Real-Life Story: The Dress Rehearsal 🎭

A theatre company doesn't perform for the first time on opening night. They hold **full dress rehearsals**: same costumes, same lighting, same timing, no stopping. Mistakes in rehearsal are **good**: they show what to fix before it matters.

Your mock exams are your dress rehearsals. **Same length, same time limit, no notes, no pausing.**

### Mock Exam Rules (make it real)
1. **90 minutes**, one sitting, timer visible.
2. **No notes, no phone, no pausing.** Use only a blank sheet (like the exam whiteboard).
3. Answer **every** question, guessing if needed.
4. **Flag** questions you're unsure about (write the number down), then review them if time remains.
5. Mark it afterwards using the answer key and record your results in the tracker below.

> ⚠️ These mocks are multiple-choice only. For PBQ practice, redo **Chapter 50**. The real exam also includes PBQs, so practise both.

### The Mistake Review (more valuable than the score!)

For **every** wrong (and every guessed) answer, write in your mistake journal:
1. **What the question was really asking** (the keyword: BEST, FIRST, MOST likely?)
2. **Why the right answer is right**, in your own words
3. **Why your answer was wrong**: knowledge gap, misread question, or a trap?
4. **The chapter to re-read**

```
   Type of mistake        Fix
   ─────────────────────  ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   Knowledge gap          Re-read the chapter, redraw its figure, make flashcards
   Misread the question   Slow down; read the LAST line first; underline NOT/FIRST
   Fell for a distractor  Compare the two final options against EVERY clue
   Changed a right answer Only change answers when you find a clear reason
```

### Readiness Check ✅
Book (or keep) your exam date when:
- ✅ You score **85%+ on two different** full mock exams (Mock 2 and Mock 3 here, or other quality practice exams),
- ✅ **No domain** is below **75%**,
- ✅ You can rewrite the **brain-dump sheet** in under 5 minutes,
- ✅ You can complete the Chapter 50 PBQs in about **4 minutes each**.

If not: **move the exam date** (CompTIA/Pearson usually allow rescheduling in advance; check the current policy). Rescheduling is far cheaper than a retake.

## 📖 Part 4 — Exam Day 🎯

### The Night Before 🌙
- **Stop studying by early evening.** Cramming late hurts more than it helps.
- Prepare: **government photo ID** (name must match your registration exactly), confirmation email, directions/travel time, or a tested OnVUE setup (system test done, clean desk, quiet room).
- **Sleep 7–8 hours.**

### Exam-Day Checklist

## 🖼️ Figure 51.4 — Checklist

```
   ☐ Eat a normal meal; bring water for before/after
   ☐ ID ready (and a second ID if required)
   ☐ Arrive 30 minutes early (test center), or log in 30 minutes early (OnVUE)
   ☐ Phone off and stored; pockets empty
   ☐ Use the restroom before starting
   ☐ At the start: brain dump on the whiteboard/notes (if permitted)
   ☐ Glance at PBQs → flag long ones → do multiple-choice → return to PBQs
   ☐ Pace check: ~question 45 by minute 40–45
   ☐ Last 10 minutes: answer EVERY remaining question (no blanks!)
```

### Time Plan

```
   0 ────── 5 ─────────────────────────── 60 ──────────────── 80 ──────── 90 min
   │ glance │ multiple-choice questions    │ flagged PBQs     │ review     │
   │ at PBQs│ (~45-50 s each)              │                  │ & no blanks│
```

### During the Exam: Staying Calm 🧘
- **Hard question?** Make your best guess, **flag** it, move on. One question is worth ~1%.
- **Panic?** Close your eyes, take **3 slow breaths** (4 seconds in, 4 hold, 6 out), then continue.
- **Strange wording?** Read the **last line** first, find the **keyword** (BEST/FIRST/NOT), eliminate two options, decide between the last two using the **scenario clues**.
- **Don't change answers** unless you find a clear reason.

## 📖 Part 5 — After the Exam 🎉

- You'll see a **pass/fail result** on screen right away; the score report lists objectives where you lost points.
- **If you pass:** congratulations! 🎉 Your certification is valid for **3 years**. Keep it active with **continuing education (50 CEUs)** or by passing a higher-level CompTIA exam. Add it to your CV and LinkedIn.
- **If you don't pass:** it happens to many strong candidates. Use the score report's weak objectives to target study, retake the mocks, and book again (check CompTIA's current retake policy).
