## Section A — CIA, Non-Repudiation & AAA (Ch 12)

A. Authorization  B. Accounting  C. Authentication  D. Identification

**5.** Why do shared administrator accounts weaken non-repudiation?

A. Authorization  B. Availability  C. Accounting  D. Integrity
